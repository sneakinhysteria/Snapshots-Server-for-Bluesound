# Support

For help with **Snapshots for Bluesound** (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV) or **Snapshots Server for Bluesound**, please [open an issue](https://github.com/sneakinhysteria/Snapshots-Server-for-Bluesound/issues/new). Questions, problems and feature requests are all welcome. Before opening a new issue, check whether [an existing one](https://github.com/sneakinhysteria/Snapshots-Server-for-Bluesound/issues?q=is%3Aissue) already covers it.

Opening an issue needs a free GitHub account.

## What to include

- Which app or server you use, and its version (server: shown in the page footer).
- Your speakers (models) and their BluOS version.
- The setup you were switching from and to, e.g. "Home cinema → Stereo pair with sub".
- What happened, and what you expected instead.
- For a failed recall: the recall log (server: shown on the Snapshots tab after a recall).

Issues are public. Leave out IP and MAC addresses, account names and anything else you don't want to share.

These projects are independent and not made or endorsed by Bluesound or Lenbrook. For problems with the speakers themselves or the BluOS app, contact [Bluesound support](https://support.bluesound.com/).
