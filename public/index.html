CEC volume protection

Loading…
Some TVs change the soundbar volume over HDMI-CEC by themselves. The app learns repeating CEC volume changes (regular interval, bursts faster than remote clicks) per player. Detect only logs them in Activity. Undo automatic changes restores the volume when a change fits the learned pattern; remote clicks stay. Lock volume undoes every CEC volume change (use the BluOS app or Home Assistant to change it).

Volume when the TV starts

Applied a few seconds after a player switches to one of the selected inputs. With Limit to maximum, CEC volume increases above the limit are also corrected for 90 s, since TVs often send a volume shortly after power-on.
