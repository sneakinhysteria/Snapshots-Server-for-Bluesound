# Privacy Policy – Snapshots for Bluesound

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Snapshots for Bluesound ("the app") is available for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and includes a home screen widget. This policy describes what data the app handles.

## Summary

The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer receives no data from it.

## Data the app handles

- **Speaker and group setup.** The app reads speaker names, groups, inputs, volume and settings from the BluOS speakers on your local network and stores them as snapshots on your device.
- **Snapshots and server address.** Snapshots and the address of an optional Snapshots Server for Bluesound are synced between your own devices through your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **Widget.** The widget keeps a list of your snapshots in storage shared between the app and the widget on the same device.

## Network connections

The app connects only to:

- BluOS speakers on your local network, to read and restore their setup.
- A Snapshots Server for Bluesound on your local network, if you set one up. The server runs on your own hardware.

The app does not connect to any server operated by the developer. Links in the app to GitHub, the App Store or the BluOS app open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

## Local network access

iOS and tvOS ask for permission to access your local network. The app uses it only to find and control your speakers and your Snapshots server.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
